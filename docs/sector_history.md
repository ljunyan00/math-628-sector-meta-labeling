The fixed universe excludes XLRE and XLC. Consequently, the nine-ETF basket is not a complete, constant partition of the contemporary sector classification system. Its historical cross-sector correlation and dispersion describe the chosen investment vehicles.

## Material changes within the research period

- **2016 real estate separation:** XLF's underlying index removed most real estate companies in September 2016, and XLF distributed XLRE shares following a transfer of the affected holdings. A contemporaneous [SEC-filed description](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/947263/000089109217004261/e74409_424b2.pdf) describes the September 16 rebalance and distribution. The vendor file records a corporate action for XLF. The adjusted-unit engine follows the vendor series; Stage 1 does not independently reconstruct the distributed XLRE position.
- **2018 communication services reclassification:** companies moved from technology and consumer discretionary into the expanded communication services sector. S&P DJI implemented the changes at the September 24, 2018 open, while the GICS Direct implementation date differed. The distinction is documented in the [September 2018 S&P/MSCI announcement](https://www.msci.com/documents/1296102/1339060/GICS-S%26P-Dow%2BJones-Indices%2Band-MSCI-announce-updates-to%2Blist-of-companies-changing-due-to-revisions-to-the-GICS-structure-September-2018.pdf/8fc2e907-8f8f-22c7-60e2-9076d1bfe880). This changes the interpretation of XLK and XLY exposures through time.
- **2023 GICS changes:** March revisions moved payment-processing companies from technology into financials, certain business-service companies into industrials, and some retailers from discretionary into staples. [State Street's February 2023 explanation](https://www.ssga.com/library-content/story/general/etf/emea/spdr-gics-changes-incoming.pdf) documents the affected sectors and planned implementation. This is reference metadata; no 2023 strategy outcome is examined in Stage 1.

These notes identify major classification breaks, not every constituent addition, deletion or index methodology change. They should accompany final interpretation and motivate checks around structural changes. No classification event is used as a fitted predictor or to alter the predeclared opportunity rule.
